ABOUT THE FIELD GUIDE

A shared sense of touch.

Awesome-Touch is an open collection of tactile sensing research and resources, maintained by Changyi Lin and the research community.

Explore sensor hardware, tactile gloves and datasets, representations, manipulation, robot learning, simulators, products, and laboratories. Hardware research has a home here in its own right.

Curated once. Available everywhere.

The website is generated from the repository’s README. Publication years come from its citations; “added” dates record the first appearance in Git history. Available resource filters mean a link is listed in the collection, not that its contents have been independently audited. Promised releases are labeled separately.

Summaries and sensor technologies appear only with a recorded source. Missing metadata is shown explicitly. All original entries and category memberships are retained through deduplication.
